Inguqulo 1.5

Inguqulo 1.4

Khetha iziphequluli nezinhlelo zazo eziningi, ngamaphrofayela e-Firefox aqoqwe ngokohlelo axhumene nalo.

  • Kwengezwe i-Chrome Dev ne-Canary; i-Edge Beta, Dev ne-Canary; i-Brave Beta ne-Nightly; i-Vivaldi Snapshot; ne-Firefox Developer Edition ne-Nightly njengezinketho zesiphequluli ezihlukene.
  • Kwengezwe ukuvula okuvamile ezingeni lohlelo lokusebenza kwe-Opera, Arc, Orion ne-Safari Technology Preview.
  • Amaphrofayela e-Firefox agcinwa nohlelo lwawo olurekhodiwe lapho lungahlonzwa, futhi okuphindiwe okudala okutholakele kuyasuswa lapho kuseshwa futhi. Amaphrofayela angenayo imethadatha yokuxhumana ethembekile agcina indlela eyisipele ekhona.
  • Kulungiswe izindlela zamafayela zamaphrofayela e-Firefox ebezinqatshwa ngemva kokulungiswa kwezindlela zesikhashana ze-macOS.
  • Kuboniswa kuphela izinhlanganisela zezindlela zokuvula ezisekelwayo. Amawindi ayimfihlo ahlala eyizinketho ezingeni lesiphequluli; indlela eyimfihlo yephrofayela ethile ayitholakali.
