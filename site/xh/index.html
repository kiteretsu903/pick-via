01 / Khetha ngaphambi kokuvula

Isikhangeli esifanelekileyo.
Iprofayile efanelekileyo.

Khetha indawo efanelekileyo kwikhonkco ngalinye lewebhu okanye le-imeyile.

01

Ikhonkco elifanelekileyo, iprofayile efanelekileyo.

Khetha isikhangeli okanye iprofayile evuliweyo, okanye ifestile yabucala exhaswayo kwinqanaba lesikhangeli.

02

Ikhonkco le-imeyile, i-app efanelekileyo.

Khetha i-app ye-imeyile ebhalisiweyo oyifunayo endaweni yokuthumela lonke ikhonkco le-mailto: kwi-app enye engagqibekanga.

03

Khetha ngaphandle kokucotha.

Lungelelanisa izinto oqhele ukuzikhetha, uze usebenzise ukucofa kube kanye okanye indlela emfutshane yekhibhodi.

04

Nohlelo lwesikhangeli sakho.

Khetha iintlelo ezahlukeneyo zeChrome neEdge, iBrave Beta okanye Nightly, iVivaldi Snapshot, neFirefox Developer Edition okanye Nightly. Iiprofayile zeFirefox ezinonxulumano olwaziwayo nohlelo zihlala kolo hlelo.

05

Izikhangeli ezingakumbi onokukhetha kuzo.

IOpera, Arc, Orion, neSafari Technology Preview zinokhetho lwesikhangeli oluqhelekileyo. Inkxaso yeeprofayile neefestile zabucala iyahluka ngesikhangeli. Jonga inkxaso yezikhangeli →

i-app efanelekileyo.

Faka kwiMac

  1. Khuphela uze uvule iDMG.
  2. Tsala PickVia uye kuApplications.
  3. Zama ukuvula i-app kube kanye. Ukuba imacOS iyayivimba, vula Iisetingi zenkqubo → Ubumfihlo & Nokhuseleko uze ukhethe Vula nangona kunjalo.
Ukuba “Vula nangona kunjalo” ayibonakali

Emva kokusa i-app kuApplications, vula iTerminal uze usebenzise:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/PickVia.app"
