Privacy Policy

Effective August 14, 2026

Summary

PickVia does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or telemetry.

The app

  • Links are handled locally and passed directly to the browser or mail app you choose.
  • PickVia does not log or retain opened URLs, recipients, subjects, or message bodies.
  • Settings are stored locally in your Application Support folder.
  • When you grant browser-profile access, PickVia stores a local security-scoped bookmark and reads only the metadata needed to identify profiles. It does not read browsing history, cookies, sessions, saved passwords, or page content.

The website

The PickVia website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, forms, or cookies set by PickVia. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its Privacy Statement.

Third parties

Browsers, mail apps, GitHub, and other services you choose to open or visit have their own privacy practices. PickVia does not control those services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be published on this page.
