20 AUG 2026Current release

Version 1.3

DuckDuckGo is now available as a browser choice, with a private option on supported official builds.

  • Choose ordinary or private DuckDuckGo from the same chooser.
  • Private mode uses isolated disposable state, needs no extension or Accessibility permission, and is limited to supported official direct-download builds.

15 AUG 2026

Version 1.1

Email links can now open in the installed mail app you choose.

  • Pick from registered mail apps and manage their order in Mail Settings.
  • PickVia does not preview or save recipients, subjects, message bodies, or the original mail link.
